## ✦ Project Overview

This project is a personal branding exercise created to explore how
visual elements can represent a person's identity.

For this activity, I developed a simple personal brand system using a
combination of color, typography, imagery, and personal information.

The final output brings these elements together into one visual
composition that represents my personality and creative direction.

Rather than using many different visual elements, I focused on creating
a small but recognizable design system that could be used consistently
across different materials.

---

## 💭 The Idea Behind the Design

The concept of my personal brand was inspired by a playful and energetic
visual identity.

I wanted the design to feel:

- Creative
- Friendly
- Memorable
- Simple
- Confident
- Personal

The branding concept uses the name **"Badink's"** together with a
ghost-inspired mascot and the phrase:

> **"We ain't afraid of dinks."**

The visual concept gives the brand a fun personality while still
maintaining a structured and organized appearance.

The design was also connected to the theme:

> **PICKLE • PADDLE • PLAY**

which helped establish the playful character of the brand.

---

# 🎨 Color System

A limited color palette was intentionally selected to keep the branding
consistent and recognizable.

| Color | HEX Code | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 Red | `#b81d16` | Primary accent and brand emphasis |
| ⚪ White | `#ffffff` | Clean space and readability |
| ⚫ Dark Grey | `#383736` | Strong background and contrast |
| 🟤 Pastel Grey | `#cabaa9` | Supporting tone and softer visual balance |

---

## 🔴 Red — `#b81d16`

Red serves as the main accent color of the design.

It creates visual energy and immediately attracts attention to important
elements such as the brand name, headings, and selected details.

I chose red because it gives the design a confident and energetic
personality.

---

## ⚪ White — `#ffffff`

White provides contrast and breathing space.

It helps balance the stronger colors and allows text and visual elements
to remain easy to read.

White was especially useful in creating a clean background for the
color palette and typography sections.

---

## ⚫ Dark Grey — `#383736`

Dark grey was selected as the primary dark tone.

Instead of using pure black, the dark grey creates a softer appearance
while still providing strong contrast.

It was used effectively in the final section of the design to create
a bold visual ending.

---

## 🟤 Pastel Grey — `#cabaa9`

Pastel grey adds warmth and softness to the visual identity.

It complements the dark grey and red while preventing the overall design
from becoming too intense.

This color also creates a more balanced and approachable appearance.

---

# 🔤 Typography System

Typography was one of the main elements of this branding project.

I selected two display fonts:

### Marykate

**Marykate** was used for selected branding and display elements.

Its playful handwritten appearance supports the informal and creative
personality of the concept.

### Hangyaboly

**Hangyaboly** was also incorporated as a decorative display typeface.

Its expressive letterforms help reinforce the playful character of the
brand.

---

## Why These Fonts?

The typography choices were not made only for appearance.

I wanted the fonts to communicate personality.

A standard professional typeface could have made the design feel more
formal, but the purpose of this branding concept was to create something
more expressive and memorable.

The handwritten and playful characteristics of the selected fonts help
make the brand feel more personal.

---

# 🧩 Visual Elements

Several visual elements were combined to establish the identity of the
project.

### 👻 Mascot

The ghost mascot is one of the most recognizable elements in the
composition.

It gives the brand a distinct personality and helps communicate the
playful nature of the concept.

### 🏓 Paddle

The paddle reinforces the sports-related theme and connects the visual
identity with the "pickle, paddle, play" concept.

### ✍️ Handwritten Branding

The handwritten treatment of the brand name creates a more casual and
approachable personality.

### 📇 Contact Information

The final section incorporates personal information using a structured
layout with recognizable icons.

This demonstrates how the same visual identity can be extended into a
personal information or contact card.

---

# 🖼️ Final Composition

The final artwork combines the different parts of the branding system
into one continuous visual presentation.

The design includes:

1. Brand identity
2. Supporting tagline
3. Color palette
4. Typography selection
5. Personal information
6. Contact details
7. Supporting visual elements

The result is a visual guide that demonstrates how individual design
choices can work together as one identity.
